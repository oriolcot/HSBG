Fast context, not clutter.

This tool focuses on one useful question: who is visible on the public leaderboard, and at what MMR?

01 · Paste

Add BattleTags

Use a comma or a new line between names. Full BattleTags give the most precise match.

02 · Match

Pick the right queue

Choose Solo or Duos, region, and season. “Current Season” updates from Blizzard’s public leaderboard.

03 · Read

Compare the lobby

Results are sorted by public rank. Players outside the scanned portion of the board may not appear.
