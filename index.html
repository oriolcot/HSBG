A NOTE ON THE NUMBERS

Public data.
A useful perspective.

HSBG is an independent community tool built around Blizzard’s public leaderboards.

How HSBG works ↗
01

Snapshots, with a timestamp

Current leaderboards are scheduled to refresh every 30 minutes. Results show when the data was captured; a delayed refresh keeps the previous snapshot.

02

History has its limits

Archived seasons generally cover players above 8,000 MMR. A missing result means we have no match in the available data.

03

A name is not always unique

Players can share the same public name. Matching names across seasons do not always belong to the same person.
